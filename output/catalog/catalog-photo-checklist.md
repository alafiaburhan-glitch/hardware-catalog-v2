## Upload requirements

- PNG format, transparent or clean white background.
- One product only per image, centered, with no watermark or price label.
- Recommended minimum: 1600 x 1600 px; square canvas preferred.
- Use the suggested filename exactly to prevent option mismatches.
- Only send images you own or are licensed/authorized to reproduce.
